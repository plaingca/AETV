# Simpsons 60-second composite CFR evaluation

Date: 2026-08-25

The experiment is implemented by `scripts/experiment_simpsons_composite_cfr.py`
and its artifacts are in `runs/simpsons-composite-cfr-60s`.

## Selected transmitter

The selected transmitter uses:

1. the production two-pass 0.5 dB AETV clip/filter conditioner;
2. 3 dB driven CESSB processing on the delayed speech branch;
3. one of 32 pilot-transparent common AETV phases selected per GOP;
4. a 7 dB whole-composite complex-envelope CFR target with linear-phase
   filtering and overshoot correction.

The phase search reduced local peaks by 0.37 dB on average and at most 1.00 dB.
The final composite CFR operated initially on 0.768% of samples.

## PAPR and power

- selected complete-file PAPR: 7.216 dB
- selected sustained PAPR: 7.183 dB
- conditioned control sustained PAPR: 13.117 dB
- selected average power at a 100 W PEP ceiling: 18.98 W
- selected voice/data average-power split: 49.46% / 50.54%
- power in the 2.2--2.5 kHz guard: 0.008%

The control has a 16.53 dB complete-file peak at the AETV-to-drain transition;
its representative continuous-operation value away from shutdown is 13.12 dB.
The candidate controls both normal peaks and the shutdown transition.

## Paired evaluation

All 60 GOP demodulation calls returned in every profile. Severe-profile framing
success does not imply useful content.

Clean latent NMSE is 0.09710 for the control and 0.09558 for the candidate.
Clean candidate speech is 24.19 dB SI-SDR. The visual transport is effectively
unchanged; the audible tradeoff is the extra speech density from CESSB/CFR.

## Artifacts

- `simpsons_60s_candidate_clean.mp4`: source on the left audio channel and
  clean candidate recovery on the right
- `simpsons_60s_candidate_awgn6.mp4`: source versus candidate at AWGN 6 dB
- `simpsons_60s_candidate_mpp6.mp4`: source versus candidate at MPP 6 dB
- `simpsons_60s_paired_*.mp4`: control audio on the left and candidate audio on
  the right, with source/control/candidate video panels
- `simpsons_60s_candidate_channel_grid.mp4`: source plus all seven candidate
  channel profiles
- `transmit_current.wav` and `transmit_candidate.wav`: 12 kHz transmit waveforms
- `sweep.json`, `metrics.json`, and `phase_choices.json`: numeric results
- `waterfalls/simpsons_60s_*_rx_waterfall.mp4`: synchronized source, decoded,
  and three-second received-spectrum waterfall for every channel profile

The waterfall videos are generated by
`scripts/render_simpsons_channel_waterfalls.py`. They use one fixed -60 to
+10 dBr color scale across profiles, with markers at the 2.2 kHz voice edge,
2.5/2.6 kHz guard and AETV edges, and 4.9 kHz upper filter edge.

All MP4s decode completely as H.264 constrained-baseline video with AAC-LC
stereo audio and browser-compatible `yuv420p` pixel format.
