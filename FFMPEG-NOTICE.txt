Bundled FFmpeg and imageio-ffmpeg notice
========================================

The AETV portable packages include an unmodified FFmpeg executable obtained
from the platform wheel for imageio-ffmpeg 0.6.0. AETV invokes this separate
executable to read source videos and save received video.
